Someone is playing guitar or another handpan, and you want to join in. {{listen}} shows you which chords they play, and where those notes are on your instrument.

In Oakhum, {{listen}} always means music around you. When you play your own notes for set-up it is {{setup-listen}}, and for one note at a time it is the {{tuner}}.

## Start listening {#steps}

1. Open the {{chords}} tab and, under the picture, tap {{listen}}.
2. Tap the Listen button. Your browser asks to use the microphone. Tap Allow.
3. Put the phone one or two metres from the instrument that is playing.
4. The chords Oakhum hears show up in order, and their notes light up on your instrument.
5. Tap Stop when you are done.

## Get the best result {#tips}

- One instrument at a time works best.
- A quiet room helps. Talking and other music confuse it.
- Let chords ring. Very fast playing is hard to follow.

## It shows nothing {#nothing}

Hold the phone closer, about a hand away, and check that the browser may use the microphone. See [The microphone does not work](/help/troubleshooting#microphone).

## It shows the wrong chord {#wrong}

Listening is not perfect yet, and you can help. Under the list, tap {{send-recording}}. See [It heard the wrong note](/help/it-heard-the-wrong-note#report).

## Is the sound sent anywhere? {#privacy}

No. It is worked out on your phone. See [The microphone and your privacy](/help/microphone-and-privacy).
