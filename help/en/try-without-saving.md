You can open any handpan scale, tongue drum, kalimba, ocarina or flute and try it. Nothing is saved and you do not need an account.

## Open a tuning {#steps}

1. On the first screen, tap the link to play a popular tuning. If you already have instruments, tap {{try}} on {{my-instruments}}.
2. Choose the kind of instrument at the top.
3. Tap a tuning. The popular ones come first, and there is a row to see every scale.
4. Tap a note or a chord to hear it.

An instrument you have not saved shows Not saved at the top. Tap {{save}} if you want to keep it.

## Open a tuning with a link {#links}

Every tuning has its own web address, for example oakhum.com/handpan/d-kurd. You can bookmark it or send it to a friend. See [Share an instrument](/help/sharing).
