You can share a link to a tuning or a song that anyone can open, or your own instrument with its own code.

## Send a link to a tuning {#link}

1. Open the instrument.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right, then {{copy-link}}.
3. Paste the link into a message.

With a chord chosen on the {{chords}} tab, the link opens that chord. The person who gets the link does not need an account or an app.

## Send a song {#song}

Open the song and tap {{share-song}} at the top. The link opens the song on the same instrument.

## Share your own instrument {#code}

This is for an instrument you have saved, for example one with your own notes.

1. Open the instrument, tap the three dots, then {{share}}.
2. You get a short code, a link and a QR code.
3. Send the link, or let someone scan the QR code with their phone camera.

Anyone with the code, link or QR code sees its notes and chords. They cannot change yours. They can add it to their own instruments.

## Someone sent me a link {#received}

Tap it. The instrument opens and you can play it straight away. Tap {{save}} to add it to your own instruments.

## Can others see my playlists? {#private}

No. Sharing shows the instrument: its notes and chords. Your playlists and email are not shared.

## Keep the code with the instrument {#print}

Print the QR code and keep it in the case. Whoever plays the instrument next can open it with one scan. Makers and shops: see [Oakhum for makers and shops](/help/for-makers).
