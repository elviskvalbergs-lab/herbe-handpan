The {{notes}} tab is the fingering chart of your instrument. It is the last tab, after {{chords}}, {{songs}} and {{playlists}}.

![The Notes tab of a 12 hole ocarina](img:notes-tab)

## Read a fingering {#read}

- A filled hole is covered.
- An empty hole is open.
- A half filled hole is half covered.

Tap any note in the grid to see its fingering large, and to hear it.

## Hear the whole scale {#scale}

Tap {{scale-btn}} to hear the notes one after another. With the list next to it you can choose a scale, and only its notes are shown.

## Blow harder {#overblow}

On a tin whistle and some flutes, the higher notes use the same fingering as the lower ones. The note is marked with an arrow and the words blow harder.

## Which finger covers which hole? {#fingers}

Open the three dots menu and tap {{which-finger}}. In {{settings}} you can also switch on {{finger-labels}}.

## The fingering looks wrong for my instrument {#wrong}

Charts come from printed maker charts, but instruments differ. Tell us with {{feedback}} in {{settings}} and say which instrument you have.
