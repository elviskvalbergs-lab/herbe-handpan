{{my-instruments}} lists every instrument you have saved, with your playlists below them.

![My instruments, with one handpan saved](img:my-instruments)

## Open an instrument {#open}

Tap it. Oakhum also remembers the last one you played and opens it next time.

To come back to this list from an instrument, tap its name at the top left.

## Add another instrument {#add}

Tap {{add-instrument}} and set it up like the first one. You can have as many as you like, of any kind.

## Rename it or add a photo {#edit}

Tap the three dots on its row, then {{edit-instrument}}. A photo of the top of the instrument works best. You can also do this on the instrument's {{set-up-tab}} tab.

## Remove an instrument {#remove}

Tap the three dots on its row, then {{remove-instrument}}. Oakhum asks before it removes anything.

## I do not see this screen {#missing}

You see the welcome screen instead until you have saved an instrument. Saving needs a free account. See [Saving and accounts](/help/accounts-and-saving).
