The tuner names the note it hears. Use it to check a note on your instrument, or to find out what a note is.

## Use the tuner {#steps}

1. Tap the microphone button at the top right. Your browser asks to use the microphone. Tap Allow.
2. Play one note and let it ring.
3. A bar appears at the bottom with the name of the note and a needle.
4. Tap the cross on the bar to stop.

## Read the needle {#needle}

- Needle in the middle: the note is in tune.
- Needle to the left: the note is a little low. This is called flat.
- Needle to the right: the note is a little high. This is called sharp.

The small number is how far off it is. Up to about 10 either way is fine for most instruments.

## Can I retune my handpan with it? {#retune}

No. The tuner only tells you what it hears. A handpan or tongue drum that has gone out of tune needs a tuner, the person. Ask the maker or the shop.
