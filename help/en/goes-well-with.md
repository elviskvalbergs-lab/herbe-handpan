You do not need theory to find chords that fit. Choose one chord and Oakhum suggests the next.

## Try the suggestions {#steps}

1. On the {{chords}} tab, tap a chord.
2. Under {{goes-with}}, tap any of the small chord buttons. Each is a chord that sounds good after the one you chose.
3. Go back and forth between them. That is already music.

## Ready made progressions {#progressions}

Tap {{ready-progressions}} next to {{goes-with}}. Oakhum lists well known chord orders in the key of your instrument, all playable on it. Tap play to hear one. Tap Save to keep it as a playlist. See [Playlists](/help/playlists).

## What do the letters mean? {#letters}

Dm is short for D minor, and a letter alone, such as F, is F major. See [Words explained](/help/glossary#chord-names).

## What is a progression? {#what}

Chords in an order, played round and round. See [Words explained](/help/glossary#progression).
