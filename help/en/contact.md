## Help inside the app {#in-app}

- Tap a small question mark next to a heading. It opens the help for just that part, at the right place.
- Open the three dots menu at the top right of a screen and tap {{help}}. It opens the help for the screen you are on.
- In {{settings}}, tap {{help}} to see every topic.

Help in the app works without an internet connection. The same pages are on [oakhum.com/help](/help).

## Tell us what is wrong or missing {#feedback}

Open {{settings}} and tap {{feedback}}. It opens a short form on oakhum.com. Something confusing, wrong or missing: it all helps.

## Ask for an instrument or a feature {#request}

Use the request form on [oakhum.com/request](/request). Others can vote for a request, and we build the ones most people ask for.

## Write to us {#email}

The email address is at the bottom of every page on oakhum.com.
