## There is no sound {#no-sound}

- Turn up the volume of your phone.
- On an iPhone, check that the silent switch on the side is off.
- Tap a chord or a note once more. Browsers only allow sound after a tap.
- If you use Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, check that they are connected.

## The microphone does not work {#microphone}

If Oakhum says that microphone access was refused, the browser has blocked it.

- iPhone: open the Settings app, find your browser, and allow Microphone. Then reload Oakhum.
- Android: tap the icon at the left of the address bar, then Permissions, and allow Microphone.
- Computer: click the icon at the left of the address bar and allow the microphone.

If it says no device was found, the phone or computer has no microphone connected, or another app is using it.

## The app does not hear anything {#silent-mic}

Hold the phone closer, about a hand away from the instrument. Check that nothing covers the microphone.

## My instruments are gone {#gone}

- If you did not have an account, they were only kept until the tab closed. Set the instrument up again and tap {{save}} this time.
- If you have an account, check that you are signed in, with the same email. Open {{settings}} and look under {{account}}.

## It asks me to sign in again {#signed-out}

Type your email and the new code. Your things come back, because they are kept in your account.

## The app looks old or something is missing {#old}

Open {{settings}} and tap {{check-updates}}.

## A chord sounds different from my instrument {#sound}

The sound in the app is made by the app. It is there to show you the notes, and it does not copy the sound of your own instrument.
