Open {{settings}} from the three dots menu at the top right of an instrument, or the cog on the home screen.

![The Settings screen, with Display and Sound at the top](img:settings)

## How notes are written {#notes}

- {{note-names}}: write the black keys as sharps, like C#, or as flats, like D♭. They are the same notes. Choose the way your instrument or its sticker writes them.
- {{note-style}}: letters, C D E, or Do Re Mi.

## Dark or light {#theme}

{{theme}} can be dark, light, or follow your phone.

## Speed {#speed}

How fast chords, scales and fingerings play, everywhere in the app. See [Make it play slower or faster](/help/speed).

## Handpan {#handpan}

- {{ring-orientation}}: turn the picture so the two lowest notes are towards you.

## Fingering pictures {#fingering}

For ocarinas and flutes: show which finger covers each hole, zoom in on the holes of a flute, and bring back the small explanation of the pictures.

## Account {#account}

Sign in or out, see what is saved, export your data or delete your account. See [Saving and accounts](/help/accounts-and-saving).

## Get the newest version {#updates}

Tap {{check-updates}}. It reloads the latest app and your saved things.
