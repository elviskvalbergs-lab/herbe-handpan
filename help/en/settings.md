Open {{settings}} from the three dots menu at the top right of an instrument, or the cog on {{my-instruments}}. You only see the parts for the kinds of instrument you have or are trying.

![The Settings screen, with Notes and display at the top](img:settings)

## How notes are written {#notes}

- {{note-names}}: Auto writes each instrument the way its scale is usually written, for example B♭ on a D Kurd and F# on a D whistle. Or choose sharps, like C#, or flats, like D♭, for everything. C# and D♭ are the same note.
- {{note-style}}: letters, C D E, or Do Re Mi.

## Dark or light {#theme}

{{theme}} can be dark, light, or follow your phone.

## Playing {#playing}

- {{speed}}: how fast chords, scales and fingerings play, everywhere in the app. See [Make it play slower or faster](/help/speed).

## Handpans and drums {#handpan}

{{ring-orientation}}: turn the picture so the two lowest notes are towards you.

## Ocarinas and flutes {#fingering}

Show which finger covers each hole with {{finger-labels}}, zoom in on the holes of a flute, see {{which-finger}}, and bring back the small explanation of the pictures.

## Makers and shops {#makers}

{{makers}}: for people who make or sell instruments. See [Oakhum for makers and shops](/help/for-makers).

## Account and about {#account}

Sign in or out, see what is saved, export your data or delete your account. See [Saving and accounts](/help/accounts-and-saving). Here too are {{help}}, {{feedback}} and {{check-updates}}, which reloads the latest app and your saved things.
