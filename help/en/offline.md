## How do I make it work offline? {#how}

Sign in once while you are online. Oakhum then gets itself ready on that device. In {{settings}}, under {{account}}, the {{offline}} line tells you when it is ready.

For the easiest start without a connection, also add Oakhum to your home screen. See [Add Oakhum to your home screen](/help/install).

## What works offline? {#what}

Everything you have saved: your instruments, their chords, fingerings, playlists and the songs. The {{tuner}} and {{listen}} work too, because the sound is worked out on your device. The help pages in the app work offline as well.

## What does the Offline label mean? {#pill}

A small {{offline}} label at the top means the app has no connection right now. You can keep playing. Anything you change is kept on the device and sent to your account when the connection is back.

## It does not work offline without an account {#guest}

Without an account you can keep playing in the page you have open, but you cannot open the app again without a connection. A free account takes a few seconds. See [Saving and accounts](/help/accounts-and-saving).

## I changed things on two devices {#two-devices}

Each device sends its changes when it is online, and gets the changes from the others. If something looks old, open {{settings}} and tap {{check-updates}}.
