Oakhum uses the microphone in four places: {{setup-listen}} during set-up, the {{tuner}}, {{listen}} on the {{chords}} tab, and {{song-wait}} in a song.

## Is the sound sent to Oakhum? {#sent}

No. The sound is worked out on your phone or computer and is not sent anywhere.

## When is the microphone on? {#when}

Only after you tap a button that starts it, and only until you stop it or leave that screen. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows that it is on.

## Does Oakhum record me? {#recording}

While it listens, Oakhum keeps a short recording in the memory of your browser. This is so that you can send it to us if it heard your instrument wrong. The recording never leaves your device unless you send it, and it is gone when you close the page.

## What happens if I send a report? {#report}

We receive what you wrote, the notes Oakhum heard, and the recording if you leave that switch on. The recording may also catch voices in the room, and you can listen to it before you send. A photo and your email are optional. We use reports only to make listening better, and keep them until the problem is fixed, at most a year.

## How do I take the permission away? {#revoke}

In your browser's settings for this site, set the microphone to blocked or ask. Oakhum then asks again next time.

## Where is the full privacy notice? {#notice}

On [oakhum.com/privacy](/privacy). See also [Your data and privacy](/help/privacy).
