You can play without an account. Without one, what you set up is gone when you close the page.

## What an account gives you {#why}

- Your instruments and playlists are kept.
- They are the same on your phone, tablet and computer.
- The app works without an internet connection. See [Use Oakhum offline](/help/offline).

It is free. We only keep your email and what you save.

## Create an account or sign in {#sign-in}

1. Tap {{save}} on an instrument, the button that starts with {{keep-this}} at the end of set-up, or the bookmark on a chord. You can also open {{settings}} and tap {{sign-in}}.
2. Type your email and tap {{send-code}}.
3. Open the email from Oakhum. Type the code from it into the app, or tap the link in the same email.

There is no password. Next time, on a new device, you sign in the same way. Whatever you were saving when you signed in is saved straight away.

## I tapped Save and it asked for my email {#asked}

That is the same thing: saving needs an account. Tap {{not-now}} if you just want to keep playing.

## The code does not arrive {#no-code}

Wait a minute and look in your spam folder. Check the email address for typing mistakes, then ask for a new code.

## Sign out {#sign-out}

Open {{settings}} and tap {{sign-out}}. This device forgets your things. Your account keeps them, and they come back when you sign in again.

## Get a copy of my data {#export}

Open {{settings}} and tap {{export}}. You get everything in your account as one file.

## Delete my account {#delete}

Open {{settings}} and tap {{delete-account}}. Your instruments, playlists and settings are deleted from our server and from this device. This cannot be undone, so export your data first if you want a copy.
