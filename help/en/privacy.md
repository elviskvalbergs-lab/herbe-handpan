This is the short version. The full [privacy notice](/privacy) is on oakhum.com.

## What do you keep if I have no account? {#guest}

We do not ask who you are. What you set up stays in your browser until you close the tab. We count visits and taps to see which parts of the app are used, without cookies and without storing an id on your device.

## What do you keep if I have an account? {#account}

Your email address and what you save: instruments, playlists and settings. They are stored in the EU.

## What about the microphone? {#microphone}

The sound is worked out on your device and is not sent anywhere. See [The microphone and your privacy](/help/microphone-and-privacy).

## Do you sell data or show adverts? {#ads}

No. Oakhum does not sell your data and shows no adverts.

## How do I get or delete my data? {#delete}

In {{settings}}: {{export}} gives you a copy, and {{delete-account}} removes everything. See [Saving and accounts](/help/accounts-and-saving#delete).
