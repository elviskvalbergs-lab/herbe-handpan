The {{chords}} tab is where most people spend their time. It only shows chords that your instrument really has the notes for.

![The Chords tab with D Minor chosen](img:chords)

## Play a chord {#play}

1. Open the {{chords}} tab.
2. Tap a chord in the list. Its notes light up on the picture and you hear it.
3. Play the lit notes on your own instrument.
4. Tap the play button to hear it again.

On an ocarina or flute you can only play one note at a time. So Oakhum plays the notes of the chord one after another, and shows the fingering for each. Tap 1, 2 or 3 to see each fingering.

## Why does it only show a few chords? {#main-chords}

Oakhum starts with the main chords: the ones that fit your scale best. Under the filters there is a line with the number of chords. Tap {{show-all}} there to see every chord your notes can make.

## Find a certain chord {#filters}

- Tap a letter in the row above the list to see only chords that start on that note.
- Tap {{chords-filters}} to choose by the number of notes or the kind of chord, such as Major or Minor.

## Keep a chord {#keep}

Tap the heart next to a chord to add it to a playlist. The same chord can go in more than once. See [Playlists](/help/playlists).

## More about one chord {#more}

Tap the three dots on the chosen chord. On a handpan, tongue drum or kalimba you can hear the scale, copy a link to the chord, and see chords that share notes with it. On an ocarina or flute you can move a note to another octave.

## What is a chord? {#what}

A few notes that sound good together. See [Words explained](/help/glossary#chord).
