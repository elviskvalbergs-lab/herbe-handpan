The name of the scale is usually on the sticker, the box or the receipt. It looks like D Kurd, or C major, 17 keys.

![Pick the note in the middle, then the scale](img:setup-scale)

## Pick your scale {#steps}

1. Tap your instrument, then {{setup-know}}. On a kalimba the button says {{know-key}}.
2. Choose the first letter of the scale name. On a handpan this is the note in the middle. On a tongue drum it is the lowest note. On a kalimba it is the key.
3. Choose your scale. The most common ones come first, each with its notes, so you can compare them with your instrument.
4. If yours is not there, tap {{show-all}} to see every scale that starts on that note.
5. Oakhum shows your instrument and a first chord. Tap {{first-chord}}.

## I cannot find my scale {#not-found}

Go back and use {{setup-listen}} instead. Oakhum will listen and use your notes just as you play them. See [Set up by playing your notes](/help/set-up-by-playing).

## My instrument has notes underneath {#bottom-notes}

Pick the scale first, then make your own copy and add the notes underneath. See [Change the notes of your instrument](/help/change-your-notes#bottom).
