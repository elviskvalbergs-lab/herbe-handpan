If you make or sell instruments, Oakhum helps your buyers keep playing after the first week. There is more on [oakhum.com/makers](/makers).

## How do I start? {#start}

1. In the app, open {{settings}} and tap {{makers}}.
2. Tap {{create-maker}}. You sign in with an email code.
3. Fill in your name, logo, story and the instruments you make. We look at each request before a page goes public.

## What do I get? {#what}

- Your page on oakhum.com, with your name, logo, story and instruments.
- A code for every instrument: a link, a QR card or an NFC tag, one per model or one per serial number.
- Made by you on the buyer's screen, with your logo and a link to your website.
- Counts of how codes get used: scans, saves and chords played. Counts only, never who.

## How does the buyer open it? {#buyer}

They scan the code or tap the tag, and their own instrument opens. They need no app store and no account.

## I sell a standard scale {#standard}

Every scale in Oakhum also has its own page and link, for example oakhum.com/handpan/d-kurd. Open the scale, tap the three dots and {{copy-link}}. Link it from your shop so people can hear a scale before they choose it.

## My scale is not in the list {#missing-scale}

Set it up as your own notes and give that a code. Or ask us on [oakhum.com/request](/request) and we add it.

## Can I use an NFC tag? {#nfc}

Yes. Steel blocks a normal tag, so on a handpan or tongue drum put it on the case or a card, or use an on-metal tag. A QR code works on every phone.

## What does it cost? {#cost}

Nothing, for you and for your buyers.

## Can a buyer change the instrument I shared? {#read-only}

No. A shared instrument is read only. The buyer can add a copy to their own instruments and change that.

## Without a maker page {#without}

You can also share any saved instrument with {{share}}: a code, a link and a QR code, without your name on it. See [Share an instrument or a song](/help/sharing).
