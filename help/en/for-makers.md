If you make or sell instruments, Oakhum helps your buyers keep playing after the first week. There is more on [oakhum.com/makers](/makers).

## How do I give a buyer their instrument? {#how}

1. Set the instrument up in the app: pick the tuning from the list, or enter your own notes, bottom notes included.
2. Save it. This needs a free account.
3. Open the three dots menu and tap {{share}}. You get a code, a link and a QR code.
4. Print the QR code on a card or the certificate, and put it in the case.

The buyer scans it and their own instrument opens. They need no app store and no account.

## I sell a standard scale {#standard}

Then you do not need to save anything. Every scale in Oakhum has its own page and link, for example oakhum.com/handpan/d-kurd. Open the scale, tap the three dots and {{copy-link}}. Link it from your shop so people can hear a scale before they choose it.

## My scale is not in the list {#missing-scale}

Set it up as your own notes and share that. Or ask us on [oakhum.com/request](/request) and we add it.

## Can I use an NFC tag? {#nfc}

Yes, write the link to the tag. Steel blocks a normal tag, so on a handpan or tongue drum put it on the case or a card, or use an on-metal tag. A QR code works on every phone.

## What does it cost? {#cost}

Nothing, for you and for your buyers.

## Can it show my brand? {#brand}

Not yet. If that would help you, write to us.

## Can a buyer change the instrument I shared? {#read-only}

No. A shared instrument is read only. The buyer can add a copy to their own instruments and change that.
