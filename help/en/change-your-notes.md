The {{pan}} tab shows your instrument and lets you change it. On a tongue drum the tab is called {{drum-tab}}, and on a kalimba {{keys-tab}}.

![The Pan tab of a D Kurd handpan](img:pan-tab)

## Change a note {#change}

1. Open the {{pan}} tab.
2. Tap {{customize}}. Oakhum makes your own copy of the scale, so the original stays as it was.
3. Tap a note to remove it, or tap a plus sign to add one there.
4. Choose the note from the list, or let Oakhum listen while you play it.
5. Give it a name if you like, then tap {{save}}.

## Add notes underneath a handpan {#bottom}

Use the plus and minus buttons under Bottom shell to say how many notes are underneath. Then tap each plus sign on the picture to set its note.

## Turn the picture round {#rotate}

The round arrow on the picture turns the ring, so that the two lowest notes are towards you. You can also set this once for every handpan in {{settings}}, under {{ring-orientation}}.

## Tongue drum: lowest note in the middle or not {#tongue-drum}

On the {{drum-tab}} tab, a switch chooses between the lowest note in the middle and all tongues round the edge. Pick the one that looks like your drum.
