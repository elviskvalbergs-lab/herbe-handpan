The last tab of a handpan shows the instrument itself. Before you save it, the tab is called {{pan}}; on a tongue drum {{drum-tab}}, on a kalimba {{keys-tab}}. Once it is in {{my-instruments}}, the tab is called {{set-up-tab}}, and its name and photo are there too.

![The Pan tab of a D Kurd handpan](img:pan-tab)

## Change a note {#change}

A scale from the list stays as its makers tune it. To change it, make your own copy.

1. Open the last tab.
2. Tap {{own-copy}}. The original stays as it was.
3. Tap a note to remove it, or tap a plus sign to add one there.
4. Choose the note from the list, or let Oakhum hear it while you play it.
5. Give it a name if you like, then tap {{save}}.

## Move notes to where they are on yours {#move}

On your own copy, tap {{move-notes}} and drag the notes to their places, so the picture looks like your instrument.

## Add notes underneath a handpan {#bottom}

On your own copy, use the plus and minus buttons under Bottom shell to say how many notes are underneath. Then tap each plus sign on the picture to set its note.

## Turn the picture round {#rotate}

The round arrow on the picture turns the ring, so that the two lowest notes are towards you. You can set this once for every handpan in {{settings}}, under {{ring-orientation}}.

## Tongue drum: lowest note in the middle or not {#tongue-drum}

On your own copy, a switch chooses between the lowest note in the middle and all tongues round the edge. Pick the one that looks like your drum.

## Rename it or add a photo {#name}

On the {{set-up-tab}} tab of a saved instrument, type a new name or add a photo. A photo of the top works best.
