Navigation wireframes

Phone frames (390 px wide in the app) for docs/navigation-redesign.md. Grey frame: today. Black frame: proposed. Boxes are controls; every instrument gets the same screen with its own picture.

1. Tabs: Songs gets its own tab

At the review the tab called Playlists held four things, with Songs, the content a beginner understands best, third. The owner has since merged playlists and progressions into one list; this moves Songs out into its own tab.

2. Chords: one thing at a time

The card answers “what is this chord and what next”. Everything set once or used rarely is one tap away, in ⋯ or Filters.
