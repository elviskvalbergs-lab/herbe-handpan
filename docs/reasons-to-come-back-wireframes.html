Reasons to come back: wireframes for release 1

Revised after review round 1. Phone size, 390 px wide. Colours, type and sizes are the app's own tokens (styles.css, light theme); every part is a shared one (uiRow, uiSheet, uiSeg, .banner, .badge, the toast). The tabs, the player's pinned controls and Wait for me are as the app has them now. The instrument pictures are placeholders for the app's drawings. Songs, keys and note counts are real fits from js/songs.js on a D Kurd handpan and a 12-hole alto C ocarina; the order of the first five is illustrative. See docs/reasons-to-come-back.md.
