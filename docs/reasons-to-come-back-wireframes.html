Reasons to come back: wireframes for release 1

Phone size, 390 px wide. Colours, type and sizes are the app's own tokens (styles.css, light theme); every part is a shared one (uiRow, uiSheet, uiSeg, .banner, .badge). The instrument pictures are placeholders for the app's drawings. Songs, keys and note counts are real fits from js/songs.js on a D Kurd handpan and a 12-hole alto C ocarina; their order in "first five" is illustrative. The Songs tab and the wait mode come from other sessions and are drawn only as far as this design needs them. See docs/reasons-to-come-back.md.
